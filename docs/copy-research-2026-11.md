# Landing copy research, November 2026

Why the landing page speaks to everyone who runs social (brands, creators, marketing teams, freelancers, agencies), and why the hero asks for an email instead of a website.

## What competitors lead with

| Product | Hero headline | Primary CTA | Under the CTA |
|---|---|---|---|
| Metricool | Plan, measure, and manage it all in one tool that saves time and gives you full control over your social media. | Create your free account | Partner badges, logos |
| Buffer | Your social media workspace | Get started for free (email field) | No credit card needed. Free forever. |
| Hootsuite | Rated #1 in social media management | Start your free trial / Request a demo | Logos |
| Sprout Social | Powerful tools for social media management | Try free for 30 days (email field) / Tour the product | No credit card required |
| Agorapulse | The AI Social Media Platform Built for Business Results | Try it now / Take a tour | Free for 30 days, G2 rating |
| Vista Social | Get started with the #1 AI SMM | Start your free trial / Request a demo | Trusted by teams at |

Patterns: a category word in the headline, "all in one place" and "save time" as the promise, an email or free signup as the main CTA, a tour or demo as the second CTA, a trust line about cost under the button. None of them uses a website audit as the main CTA.

## What paid users complain about

- Price that grows per seat and per add-on, and surprise renewals (Sprout Social reviews; Reddit threads on Hootsuite and Sprout).
- Posts that fail without notice and accounts that disconnect (Hootsuite and Buffer reviews on Capterra).
- Reporting and proving results: 69% of social teams feel more pressure to prove ROI and only 37% find it easy (HubSpot 2026).
- Keeping up: 45% of marketers say producing quality content consistently is their top challenge (HubSpot 2026).
- Replies: most consumers want a brand to reply within 24 hours (Sprout Social).
- Heavy dashboards and too many tools (Hootsuite reviews, SocialBu).

## What ZieAds says that they cannot

- It tells you what to do next and does it after you approve (they show dashboards).
- Every post is checked after it goes out and retried up to 3 times (their users report silent failures).
- Priced per brand, not per seat (their users complain about per seat pricing).
